## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, high-clarity personal identity crafted for modern digital creators, senior product leaders, and software craftspeople. Its aesthetic blends contemporary minimalism with precise corporate elegance, emphasizing editorial typographic rhythm, generous negative space, and tactful micro-interactions.

### Emotional Target
The interface projects authority, pristine attention to detail, and approachable warmth. It balances rigorous technical credibility with polished presentation to build immediate trust with prospective clients, collaborators, and executive recruiters.

### Visual Architecture
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, open, and luminous. Interfaces rely on pure white backdrops grounded by soft off-white surface layers.
- **Rhythm:** Disciplined vertical pacing, restrained hairline containment, and curated soft elevations rather than heavy skeuomorphic treatments.
- **Tone:** Articulate, refined, and deliberate. Decoration is always subordinate to work samples, case studies, and career milestones.

## Colors

The color palette centers on high-contrast slate neutrals paired with tailored optical blues, allowing imagery and case-study content to take center stage without visual clutter.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#2563EB`):** Applied to primary calls to action, high-priority interactive cues, active navigation states, and focal link typography.
- **Secondary Tint (`#EFF6FF`):** Reserved for interactive pill fills, badge backgrounds, selected card states, and subtle focus glows.
- **Interactive State / Hover (`#1D4ED8`):** The standard pressed and hover shift for all primary action surfaces and links.

### Neutral System & Surface Distribution
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary view layer for high optical contrast.
- **Surface Muted (`#F8FAFC`):** Alternating section strips, nested code wrappers, project card backgrounds, and panel insets.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure for card wrappers, dividers, and input borders.
- **Text Dominant (`#0F172A`):** Display headers, titles, and emphasis text.
- **Text Body (`#334155`):** Standard prose, lead summaries, and primary metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Captions, timestamps, inactive states, and tertiary descriptions.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemporary, rounded geometric character of Plus Jakarta Sans for structural headlines and actionable tags with the neutral reading rhythm of Inter for body content and detailed project descriptions.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with negative letter tracking to create tight, punchy, editorial headlines across hero intros and showcase titles.
- **Body & Longform:** Set in Inter at 400 weight with generous line-height ratios (1.5–1.55) to maximize readability across case study paragraphs, experience lists, and project summaries.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans at 600 weight with slightly positive tracking to maintain punchy clarity inside badges, metadata labels, and form tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at a max-width container of `1200px` to maintain optimal line lengths and balanced margins on wide displays.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure. Page margins drop to `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), and gutters to `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Project grids stack into a single column.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters and margins. Showcase cards form a balanced 2-column distribution.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `gutter-desktop` (2rem / 32px) and generous outer canvas margins (`margin-desktop`). Enables asymmetrical 8/4 split layouts for case studies, bio sidebars, and multi-card metric grids.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical pacing between major portfolio chapters follows `space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` (80px) for section isolation.
- Internal component spacing uses strict multiples: `space-xs` (4px) for micro tag padding, `space-sm` (8px) for icon gaps, `space-md` (16px) for compact card padding, and `space-lg` (24px) for standard card enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, instead using crisp structural borders paired with ultra-diffused, ambient slate drop shadows to achieve layered clarity.

### Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Flat Base):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#F8FAFC`) flat backgrounds with no shadow. Structural definition relies solely on a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Card):** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Enclosed by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Used for case study preview cards, timeline milestones, and testimonial blocks.
- **Tier 2 (Hover & Active Overlay):** `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Paired with a subtle border tint shift (`#CBD5E1`) and a 2px upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Menus & Modals):** `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 15px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with a frosted backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate corner radii to maintain a friendly yet professional aesthetic. Base inputs and interactive components use smooth geometric curvature, while larger project containers use generous corner sweeps.

### Corner Radius Scale
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form controls, small badges, and action dropdown items.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary buttons, interactive pill filters, and status tags.
- **Cards & Visual Containers (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`, 1.5rem to 2rem / 24px–32px):** Case study imagery wrappers, hero showcase banners, and credential blocks.
- **Full Rounded (`rounded-full`):** Avatar headshots, indicator dots, and floating icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-md` type token, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, `rounded-lg`. Hover shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a subtle focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. Hover changes background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Accent Soft:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, borderless. Hover shifts to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Skill / Tech Pill:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#2563EB`, 1px border of `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. Radius is fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding, set in `label-sm`.
- **Availability / Status Indicator:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#E2E8F0`, featuring a 6px circular green or blue pulse beacon alongside `label-sm` text in `#334155`.

### Project & Case Study Cards
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border of `#E2E8F0`, corner radius of `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), Tier 1 shadow.
- **Content Flow:** High-aspect 16:10 image cover nested with internal `space-md` margin, followed by category badges, `headline-sm` title, short synopsis in `body-sm`, and link footer with directional micro-icon.
- **Hover Behavior:** Translates up by 2px, shadow ascends to Tier 2, and border shifts to `#CBD5E1` over a 200ms ease-out curve.

### Timeline & Experience Lists
- **Structure:** Left-aligned 2px vertical guide stroke in `#E2E8F0` with circular junction nodes in `#2563EB` encased by `#FFFFFF` rings.
- **Content Group:** Date range set in `caption` (`#64748B`), role title in `title` (`#0F172A`), organization in `body-md` (`#2563EB`), and impact bullet items in `body-sm` (`#334155`).

### Input Fields & Contact Controls
- **Input & Textarea:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography uses `body-md` with placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`, transitions over 150ms.

### Metric & Stat Blocks
- **Card Format:** Background `#F8FAFC`, corner radius `1rem`, padding `space-lg`.
- **Content:** Big numeric value set in `display-mobile` (`#0F172A`), followed by concise label set in `label-sm` (`#64748B`).
